# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product overview
KhmerFood is a bilingual online platform for discovering Cambodian traditional food and learning how to cook it using images and written instructions.

## 2. Vision
Preserve and promote Cambodian culinary heritage through an accessible digital recipe library.

## 3. Target users
- Cambodian home cooks and families
- Beginners learning to cook
- International visitors interested in Cambodian cuisine
- Students and food enthusiasts researching Khmer food

## 4. Goals
- Make Khmer recipes easy to discover.
- Explain ingredients, quantities and cooking steps clearly.
- Make content available in Khmer and English.
- Work well on mobile, tablet and desktop.

## 5. MVP features
| Feature | Priority | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | High | Hero, search, featured recipes, categories, latest recipes |
| Recipe listing | High | Responsive cards, pagination |
| Search | High | Search Khmer and English recipe names |
| Categories | High | Browse/filter recipes by category |
| Recipe details | High | Description, time, servings, difficulty |
| Ingredients | High | Names, quantities, units, optional images |
| Cooking steps | High | Ordered instructions and step images |
| Localization | High | Khmer and English UI/content |
| SEO | Medium | Metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap |
| Responsive UI | High | Mobile-first layout |

## 6. Suggested categories
- Soups and curries (ម្ហូបសម្ល)
- Stir-fried dishes (ម្ហូបឆា)
- Grilled dishes (ម្ហូបអាំង)
- Rice dishes (ម្ហូបបាយ)
- Noodles (មី និងគុយទាវ)
- Desserts (បង្អែម)

## 7. Initial content
Start with 15–20 reviewed recipes. Suggested candidates include Fish Amok, Beef Lok Lak, Samlor Korko, Nom Banh Chok, Kuy Teav, Bai Sach Chrouk, Samlor Machu, Prahok Ktis, Cha Kroeung, Num Ansom and Num Krok. Have knowledgeable Khmer cooks review ingredients, quantities, regional variations and translations before publication.

## 8. Out of scope
No user accounts, AI assistant, videos, payments, comments, social networking, ordering or native mobile app in the MVP.

## 9. Success criteria
- Visitors can browse published recipes without signing in.
- Search works across Khmer and English names.
- Category filtering and pagination work.
- Recipe details show complete ingredients and ordered steps.
- Language switching preserves the current recipe/category route.
- Main journeys work on mobile and desktop.
- No draft recipes appear in public endpoints.
